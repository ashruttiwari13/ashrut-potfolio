CONTROLS & COMBAT

DESKTOP CONTROLS:

• Mouse Pointer : 360° Free Aim (dynamic crosshair & trajectory guide)

• Left Click (LMB) / F : SHOOT BULLETS directly into enemies!

• Right Click (RMB) : Special Triple Crystal Burst (Uses SP)

• A / D or ← / → : Move Left & Right

• W / Space / ↑ : Jump & Double Jump (Press twice in mid-air)

• E : Interact with Doors & Checkpoints

• Esc : Pause / Resume

MOBILE TOUCH CONTROLS:

• Tap Canvas : Direct 360° touch aim & bullet fire

• ◀ / ▶ Buttons : Move Left & Right

• ▲ Button : Jump & Mid-air Double Jump

• ⚔️ Button : Shoot Crystal Bullets

• ✨ Button : Special Triple Crystal Burst

• Orientation : Seamlessly adapts to Portrait & Landscape

ADVENTURE GOALS:

• Light Campfires to save your active checkpoint

• Find the hidden Ancient Relic in the forest for the True Ending!

• Collect the Dungeon Key to unlock the Crystal Guardian's chamber
