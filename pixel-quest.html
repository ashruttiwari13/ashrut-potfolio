HOW TO PLAY

DESKTOP CONTROLS:

• A / D or ← / → : Move Left & Right

• W / Space / ↑ : Jump (Hold for higher jumps)

• Left Mouse Click (LMB) : Sword Melee Slash

• Right Mouse Click (RMB) : Special Crystal Blast (Uses SP)

• E : Interact with Doors & Checkpoints

• Esc : Pause Game

OBJECTIVES:

• Defeat monsters & collect crystal shards for high score

• Light Campfires to save your checkpoint

• Find the hidden Ancient Relic in the forest for the True Ending!

• Retrieve the Dungeon Key to open the Crystal Guardian's chamber
