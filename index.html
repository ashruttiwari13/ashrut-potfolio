HAUNTED HOUSE

THE ESCAPE · 3D SURVIVAL HORROR

You wake up locked inside the decrepit mansion of THE KEEPER. She hears every sound you make. Explore the house, search drawers, locate keys and tools, disarm the front door locks, and escape before your 5 Days expire.

Select Difficulty:

SURVIVAL MANUAL

FIRST-PERSON CONTROLS:

• W / A / S / D : Walk through the corridors

• MOUSE : Look around (Click game screen to lock mouse)

• LEFT SHIFT : Sprint (Drains stamina)

• C / CTRL : Crouch (Sneak under beds and crawlspaces)

• E / LEFT CLICK : Interact with doors, drawers, items, and hiding spots

• F : Toggle Flashlight on / off

• G : Drop carried item (Creates a noise lure!)

• ESC : Pause / Resume

SURVIVAL RULES:

1. Don't make noise: Dropping items or knocking over dishes will alert The Keeper to investigate.

2. Hide when she's near: Press [E] on beds or inside wardrobes to hide until she walks away.

3. 5 Days to Escape: If she catches you, you lose a day. After Day 5, she never lets you leave.
